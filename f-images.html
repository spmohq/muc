Prompt #2: Social Media Graphic (4:5)
Based on the image you just created and the ready to blog-post directly above in this conversation, generate one best-and-final finished image that visually represents that content. Treat the content already in this conversation as the complete source of truth. Don't ask me to repeat, paste, or restate the content idea, title, description, audience, topic, or strategic direction. Do not give me an image prompt, concept, explanation, variation set, or design notes. Generate the actual image. PRIMARY JOB: Create one jaw-dropping social-media graphic that makes people stop scrolling and want to read the content. The image should capture the strongest idea, mechanism, distinction, tension, or payoff from the finished content and translate it into a visually unforgettable graphic. FORMAT: Generate the image in a 4:5 portrait format optimized for social media and mobile viewing. It should feel natively suited for platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X while remaining easy to read on a phone. VISUAL STANDARD: Make it feel premium, polished, bold, modern, high-contrast, and emotionally compelling. It should look like a high-end editorial social graphic or premium promotional poster, not a generic template, low-effort quote card, cheesy stock image, or cluttered infographic. Use strong composition, clear visual hierarchy, clean spacing, dramatic depth, and a central focal point that instantly communicates the core idea. TEXT USAGE: Keep on-image text minimal and powerful. Use the exact content title as the main headline unless a shorter version is needed for visual impact, in which case preserve the meaning. You may add one short supporting line if it strengthens the image, but keep the total text tight, readable, and mobile-friendly. The text must be crisp, prominent, and easy to read on a phone. CREATIVE DIRECTION: Build the visual around a compelling metaphor, scene, object, or dramatic visual situation that expresses the idea rather than merely decorating it. If the content suggests a strong before-versus-after contrast, tension, hidden mistake, overlooked opportunity, or diagnostic insight, show that visually. If a human figure helps, use one. If a symbolic or object-based concept is stronger, use that instead. Make the image feel intelligent and emotionally charged, not random. DESIGN RULES: - Make it instantly attention-grabbing in a crowded feed - Favor one dominant idea over clutter or too many elements - Use bold typography with excellent contrast and spacing - Keep the layout clean and easy to scan on mobile - Avoid tiny text, weak contrast, generic icons, bland backgrounds, and obvious stock-photo energy - Don't include logos, watermarks, fake UI screenshots, or unnecessary platform branding - Don't turn it into a busy step-by-step infographic unless the content absolutely demands it - The final image must feel like something a serious marketer would immediately post FINAL OUTPUT RULE: Return only the finished image. Don't return text commentary, image prompts, captions, explanations, or multiple options unless needed internally to complete the image generation. The goal is one freaking stunning finished image.
